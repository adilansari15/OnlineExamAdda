# Context: Product Overview

## Product Vision
**Online Examination Adda** is a robust, responsive web application for conducting online examinations, timed quizzes, and performance analytics.

## Core Roles
1. **Student**
   - Self-registration and secure login.
   - Browse and filter published quizzes by category/subject.
   - Participate in timed exams with continuous autosave and intuitive question navigation.
   - Immediate review of submission results with score breakdown and question-by-question explanations.
   - Historical attempt tracking and category/subject performance analytics.
   - User profile management.

2. **Admin**
   - High-level metric dashboard (active students, quizzes conducted, completion rates).
   - Category and subject taxonomy management.
   - Centralized Question Bank: support for single-choice and multiple-choice questions, difficulty levels, customizable marks/negative marking, and detailed explanations.
   - Quiz creation and scheduling: duration limits, randomized question ordering, attempt windows, total marks.
   - Student management and activity auditing.
   - Comprehensive result monitoring and score exports.
   - System settings.
